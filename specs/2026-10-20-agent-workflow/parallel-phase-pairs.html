What this proves — and what it does not

dependency certainty is separate from file-level certainty

Proven

  • 45 / 45 start-dependency rows checked
  • 82 / 82 same-frontier pairs
  • 561 / 561 independent pairs, no duplicates
  • 24-round structural minimum for two workers

Not proven

  • File-level write disjointness
  • Equal phase durations
  • Phase, milestone or deployment approval

Strict-independent

  • 17 + 43 · completion-ordered
  • 36 + 43 · independent
  • 25 + 42C · independent
  • 48 + 42D, 51 + 42F · cross-frontier
