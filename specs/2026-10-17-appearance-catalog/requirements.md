# Phase 11E requirements

Sources are roadmap Phase 11E, architecture Q-3 and section 9.3, product sections 2, 4 and 12, acceptance criteria sections 4 and 12, and the onboarding agreement.

- Exactly six themes change appearance while retaining one portfolio template: Leaf, Violet, Clay, Matcha, Strawberry and Coffee. Every theme has light and dark colors. Initial mode supports light, dark and system. The three additions use the exact semantic values read from their reference-derived Figma collections.
- Choose curated fonts with local assets, fallback stacks and redistribution notices. Published assets will use the platform asset host in later implementation.
- The curated gallery contains only the faithfully imported ginger cat (`cat`) and hands (`hands`), replacing the static Leaf/Orbit/Frame proposals. Record animated WebP/still assets, SVG/CSS source, geometry, timing and digests. No decoration is the initial state and is represented by an absent decoration ID. Face Reveal stays off and no second photo is inserted.
- Document the production motion policy and its phase ownership: autoplay, independent pause/play, reduced-motion still cat/stationary hands, and a reveal toggle that does not control decoration playback. The exact source review is retained; production owner data, labels and playback controls are later integration work.
- Record explicit theme/font/mode defaults for Phase 12. Do not insert sample identity, banners or projects into drafts, or add an onboarding appearance step.
- Preserve opaque schema references and compatibility with stored drafts. Membership validation and production controls remain in their scheduled phases.
- Figma is read-only for this integration. Inspect its direction through the connected MCP. The owner separately authorized creating the Matcha/Strawberry/Coffee boards; read those collections and record provenance without additional writes. Decorations come from the owner's source repository, not newly drawn substitutes.
- Catalog approval is required by Phase 11E itself. The implementation request alone did not approve unseen choices. The owner approved the reviewed catalog and phase completion on October 17, 2026, accepting the documented generator exception; record that decision before checking off the phase.
